## Full description

SPLICE.CLUB makes collaborative creative fusion approachable. A seven-step, wallet-free Playground lets visitors learn by picking local ingredients, activating the reactor, flipping a result and following its parents. Those offline examples are clearly labelled and make no AI or chain calls. The actual product connects to the deployed SPLICE-V2 contract on Stable Studionet. Visitors can browse seeds and fusions, inspect complete lineage, and actively request wallet signatures to register a seed or fuse two creators' seeds. Pending transaction recovery avoids automatic resubmission; account/network changes invalidate the wallet connection.
